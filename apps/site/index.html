  2. Choose a local model

    Ollama and model weights are separate from the companion. Model size affects memory use and speed. Generated answers can be wrong; review their source passages and any proposed actions.

Choose what leaves your device

Local content

Local task content and memory remain until you delete them. Use the companion’s export and deletion controls. Keep recovery material safe: Bittrees cannot recover your content keys.

App and remote access

Approved publications keep their destination’s lifecycle. Remote content and metadata retention are separate deployment choices. Independent encryption review and the personal-data pilot remain open.
