Getting started with your Mac companion

Bittrees AI is a development preview for Mac. Your Mac runs the local model; this page connects to the companion after you set it up. Windows and Linux companion apps are not available yet.

  1. Prepare the Mac preview. Read the verified download instructions and Mac setup guide. The current download is for Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or newer. It is a development build, without Apple notarization or automatic updates. Ollama and model downloads are separate.
  2. Open and pair the local app. Follow its pairing screen on your Mac. That local pairing is separate from signing in here. Keep your recovery material in a safe place; Bittrees cannot recover your content keys.
  3. Connect only what you need. Each app asks for its own permissions. Signing in here does not connect Mail, CRM or other apps. To share generic task status, start remote pairing on your Mac, then use “Pair your Mac” here. Private content access requires additional setup and approval.

Before using personal data

Start with sample content. Personal-data and native Mac acceptance, signed distribution and independent encryption review remain open. Local task content and memory stay until you delete them; use the companion’s export and deletion controls. Published copies keep their destination’s lifecycle. Remote relay retention is a separate deployment decision, so do not assume it matches local retention.

Help and feedback

Report a problem or read existing reports. Reports are public: include the app version and steps using sample data, and leave out messages, recovery codes, pairing details and credentials. These help links open GitHub; this guide sends no task content with them.
