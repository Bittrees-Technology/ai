# Reviewed Mac conversation offer uploads

The selected conversation can upload its retained encrypted invitation through a saved ai.bittrees.org connection. Refresh saved offers and connections, choose a connection, review the browser and opening deadline, then acknowledge the upload. The invitation includes sharing choices, not conversation messages or local Inbox names. Browser consent remains a separate explicit review.

Each attempt is committed to the encrypted offer row before contacting the relay. An interrupted or uncertain upload is never retried automatically. Refresh and review the original saved offer again: the retry retains its exact ciphertext, message identity, sequence and deadline. History keeps the latest attempt separate from the last confirmed server observation. Server storage, receipt or deletion is not evidence that the browser approved conversation access.

Every review and confirmation rechecks the selected offer revision, current Mac consent, endpoint keys, peer proof, connection revision, destination permission and remaining lease. Losing authority while a request is in flight leaves an uncertain attempt. Stopping an offer prevents later downloads and uploads; it does not delete existing copies or revoke the underlying conversation permission.

Task storage schema31 fences older offer writers before adding optional relay history inside existing encrypted offer records. Actual compiled schema30 upgrade verification preserves old consent, ciphertext, replay fences and outgoing sequences without inventing prior upload history. Backup and restore preserve the history while locking sending authority. Owner deletion removes the offer and its history together.

This source package adds Mac sending and review controls. Browser relay-offer retrieval, acknowledgement after explicit consent, full historical replay reconciliation and conversation-message transport remain separate work. It neither installs nor activates a service. Acer news processing and the independent Mac model configuration are unchanged.
